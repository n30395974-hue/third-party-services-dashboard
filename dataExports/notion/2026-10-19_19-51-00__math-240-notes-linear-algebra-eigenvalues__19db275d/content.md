# MATH 240 Notes: Linear Algebra Eigenvalues

## Definition
A nonzero vector **v** is an eigenvector of A if A**v** = λ**v**.
## Finding eigenvalues
1. Solve det(A − λI) = 0
2. Plug each λ back in to find the null space of (A − λI)
## Example
A = \[\[2, 0\], \[0, 3\]\] → λ = 2, 3
## Exam tip
Trace = sum of eigenvalues; determinant = product of eigenvalues.
