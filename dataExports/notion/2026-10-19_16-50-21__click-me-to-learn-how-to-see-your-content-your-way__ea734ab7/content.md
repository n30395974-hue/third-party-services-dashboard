# Click me to learn how to see your content your way

Your content can be shown in countless ways within Notion. Try adding a new **view** to the To Do List database to render your content how you want.
<empty-block/>
Hover over the “Todo” and “Done” view tabs and click the <unknown_mention url="notion://custom_emoji/1f88cc90-92fd-4ce4-bfcd-25daec2ffbbe/21817954-d9c2-80e1-a9ea-007a5b1b5365" alt="custom_emoji"/> to select a new view
![](https://s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/public.notion-static.com/1f88cc90-92fd-4ce4-bfcd-25daec2ffbbe/788969ab-bfb7-42f6-b518-23d54ab4f54c/image.png)
Select **Board** (or whatever view you’d prefer to select!). This adds a new view and can always be undone.
![](https://s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/public.notion-static.com/1f88cc90-92fd-4ce4-bfcd-25daec2ffbbe/abc445a3-9369-40f7-86ca-efba662ce9c2/image.png)
Now see your tasks in a board view! Try drag and dropping tasks into done to mark them as completed.
![](https://s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/public.notion-static.com/1f88cc90-92fd-4ce4-bfcd-25daec2ffbbe/232f735d-b975-417b-aa4c-1d69a48235b1/image.png)
