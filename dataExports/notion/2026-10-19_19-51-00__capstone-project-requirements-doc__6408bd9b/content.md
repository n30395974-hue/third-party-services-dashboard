# Capstone Project: Requirements Doc

## Problem
Students waste 15+ minutes walking between floors looking for an open study room.
## Solution
StudySpot, an app that shows live room availability and lets you book in two taps.
## Requirements
1. See all library rooms and whether each is free
2. Book a room for up to 2 hours
3. Get a reminder 10 minutes before a booking ends
