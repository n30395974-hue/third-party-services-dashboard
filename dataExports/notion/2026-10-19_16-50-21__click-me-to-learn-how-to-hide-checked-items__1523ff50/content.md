# Click me to learn how to hide checked items

From the list, click the <unknown_mention url="notion://custom_emoji/1f88cc90-92fd-4ce4-bfcd-25daec2ffbbe/22417954-d9c2-808f-8850-007a942e86cb" alt="custom_emoji"/> button to change your filters.
![](https://s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/public.notion-static.com/1f88cc90-92fd-4ce4-bfcd-25daec2ffbbe/ad522c73-8087-45a9-ab24-78a815adc04a/image.png)
Select “Status”.
![](https://s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/public.notion-static.com/1f88cc90-92fd-4ce4-bfcd-25daec2ffbbe/7e0f3e50-a095-45de-8ada-125609a62227/image.png)
Then check “To Do” and “In Progress” to only see unchecked items in the list. Click “Save for everyone” to finish.
![](https://s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/public.notion-static.com/1f88cc90-92fd-4ce4-bfcd-25daec2ffbbe/8cd17aa8-5a4b-4ee0-8cb7-d4c6615dc92a/image.png)

	**Notion Tip**
	You can keep track of all sorts of other information about your to dos!
	Click “➕ Add a property” to see all the other things you can do.
</callout>
